Her Story

Her Journey

For more than 18 years, Dr. S. Banu Chitra Mam has walked a path of dedication, sacrifice, and quiet strength.

From the very first day in the classroom, Mam carried a simple belief: that every student deserves to be seen, guided, and believed in.

Through challenges, pain, and countless responsibilities, Mam never stopped showing up — for her family, for her students, and for everyone who needed her.

This is the story of a teacher who became family.
